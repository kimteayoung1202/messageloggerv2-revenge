# Hidden Channels Fix

Original authors: cloudburst and Training Dummy.

src/hiddenfix.js adapts the upstream display permission, navigation, fetch and ChannelMessages hooks to Revenge. src/channels.js supplies the locked-screen UI. Native module discovery, real-check isolation, cache restoration and function-call corrections are local adaptations. The rest of the logger and BD compatibility adapter are separate implementations.
